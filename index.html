Who We Are

Built for the moment
no one prepares you for

When an injury ends your athletic career, the world doesn't stop — but yours feels like it does. Beyond The Game was created because that moment deserves more than generic advice and a hotline number. You deserve a platform that understands what it means to lose sport, identity, community, and income all at once — and knows how to help you rebuild all of it.

Pillar One

Mental Support

Your mind needs recovery just as much as your body. Our mental support tools are built on real therapeutic frameworks — designed specifically for athletes navigating identity loss, grief, and transition.

📓

Self-Worth Journaling

Daily guided writing prompts help you rebuild self-esteem, clarify your values, and redefine who you are beyond your sport. Adapted from CBT and ACT therapeutic frameworks.

🎙️

Motivational Stories

Video testimonials, audio stories, and written narratives from athletes who faced career-ending injuries and rebuilt meaningful lives. Find someone whose story matches yours.

💬

Anonymous Chat

Stigma-free, fully anonymous chat support available 24/7. Speak openly without fear of judgment — designed for athletes who struggle to ask for help.

👥

Athlete-to-Athlete Community

A moderated peer platform where injured athletes share experiences, offer advice, and support each other — because no one understands like someone who has been there. Share your feelings freely with people who truly get it.

🩺

Online Therapist Connection

Book secure video, voice, or text sessions with licensed therapists and sports psychologists who specialize in athletic identity loss and career grief.

📊

Daily Mood Tracking

Complete a quick daily check-in on your emotional state, pain, sleep, and social connection. View your Weekly Emotional Progress Chart and share it with your therapist.
